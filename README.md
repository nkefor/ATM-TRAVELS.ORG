# ATM Travels & Pilgrimages (April-Marie’s Traveling Mary)

Full web application clone of **ATM Travels** (`atm-travels.org`), featuring:
- **Home**: Hero section, mission statement, upcoming pilgrimages & events, featured store showcase, and pilgrim reviews.
- **About Us**: Complete *April-Marie’s Traveling Mary* story, *Our Love for Children & the Family*, *Background*, *More Than a Vacation*, *Community Outreach* (*Home Based Chapels* & *The Traveling Statues*), *Children's Ministry* (*Daily Rosary* & *Seven Children*), and *Our Story*.
- **Events & Pilgrimages**: Full payment/installment tiers and interactive pilgrimage registration modal.
- **Past Events**: Photographic archive gallery.
- **Product Store**: Handcrafted botanical soaps, balms, oils, body butter, and devotional items with Grid/List toggle, single-product detail view, and slide-out shopping cart drawer.
- **Reviews**: 5-star reviews from pilgrimages and retreats (*Mother's Retreat 2026*, *Children's Prayers*, *Rosa Mystica Retreat*, *Medjugorje Pilgrimages*) plus an interactive review submission form.
- **Blog & Contact**: Journey notes and Dacula, Georgia contact inquiry form.

## Running Locally

Open `index.html` directly in your browser, or run the included PowerShell server:

```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\serve.ps1
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8080`.
